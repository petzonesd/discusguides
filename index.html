Small tank, unforgiving standards

Discus have a reputation, and it's earned. They're not hard because they're delicate specimens that keel over at the first mistake — they're hard because they demand consistency. Warm water, low organics, and steady chemistry, held for years, not weeks. Get it right and you get a fish that schools, pair-bonds, feeds its own fry off its skin, and grows into a dinner-plate-sized centerpiece with color that shifts by the week.
