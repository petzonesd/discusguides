Melon Red Discus

By Pet Zone SD Team • September 2026

The Essentials
  • Size: 6–8 inches (15–20 cm) as adults
  • Tank: 55 gallons minimum for a group, tall (20"+ height)
  • pH: 6.0–7.2
  • Temperature: 82–86°F (28–30°C)
  • Hardness: Soft to moderate (2–8 dGH)
  • Temperament: Peaceful, shoaling
  • Difficulty: Intermediate to advanced — solid color shows stress and poor water fastest

Overview

Melon Red is the solid-red member of the broader "Melon" strain family, which also includes the paler, sunnier Yellow Melon. Where Yellow Melon leans toward glowing yellow-orange, Melon Red pushes further into a warm, saturated orange-red — closer to the fruit that gives the family its name than to Marlboro Red's deeper brick tone. It's often sold and discussed alongside Marlboro Red and Rose Red as one of the three benchmark solid-red options, though genetically it belongs to the Melon lineage rather than the Marlboro line.

As with any solid-color strain, evenness and saturation — not pattern — are what separates a great specimen from a mediocre one, and both traits fade quickly under poor water or a thin diet.

Tank Setup & Water

No different from other discus strains — see the Red Turquoise guide for the complete tank, substrate, and water chemistry breakdown.

Feeding

Standard discus diet — quality pellets as a staple, with beef heart mix and carotenoid-rich foods in rotation to help intensify the red-orange tone.

Temperament & Compatibility

Standard discus shoaling behavior — peaceful, group-dependent, no special compatibility concerns.

Breeding Notes

Standard pairing and parental slime-feeding — see the Red Turquoise guide for the full walkthrough. Solid-color genetics generally breed true, but a home-bred spawn still benefits from culling toward the most even, saturated fry.

Where to Get Quality Fish

Look for a bright, uniform orange-red with no dulling or patchy fading, and clean, undamaged fins. See our sourcing guide for more.

Final Thoughts

Melon Red is an excellent pick for a keeper who wants a warmer, brighter red than Marlboro's deep saturation — same straightforward care, a genuinely vivid centerpiece fish.
