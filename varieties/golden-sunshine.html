Golden & Sunshine Discus

By Pet Zone SD Team • September 2026

The Essentials
  • Size: 6–8 inches (15–20 cm) as adults
  • Tank: 55 gallons minimum for a group, tall (20"+ height)
  • pH: 6.0–7.2
  • Temperature: 82–86°F (28–30°C)
  • Hardness: Soft to moderate (2–8 dGH)
  • Temperament: Peaceful, shoaling
  • Difficulty: Intermediate to advanced

Overview

Golden (also marketed as Sunshine or Sunshine Golden) is a solid-color farm strain built around a vivid, saturated orange-gold that covers nearly the entire body — one of the warmest, most vivid tones in the solid-color discus lineup, sitting between the yellow of Lemon Yellow and the deeper red-orange of Melon on the color spectrum. Normal dark eyes are standard on this strain; a pale, pastel version with red eyes is a separate albino expression of the same base color, covered on the Albino page under "Albino Sunshine Golden."

As with other solid-color strains, Golden is judged primarily on evenness and saturation — a fish with patchy, blotchy, or unevenly distributed gold is graded down regardless of how bright any individual section looks, and uneven color is also one of the more visible signs of stress or declining water quality in this strain specifically.

Tank Setup & Water

  • Tank: Standard discus baseline — tall, 55 gallons minimum for a growing group.
  • Substrate: Bare-bottom is common among breeders and show keepers chasing peak, even color, consistent with other solid-color strains.
  • Water chemistry: Standard discus range; stable, low-nitrate water supports consistent saturation over time.

Feeding

Standard discus diet — quality pellets as the staple, with beef heart mix and carotenoid-rich foods in rotation to help support the warm gold-orange tone, per the feeding guide.

Temperament & Compatibility

Standard discus social behavior — peaceful, shoaling, best kept in groups of six or more, fully compatible with the usual Amazon-biotope tankmates. A vivid warm-toned centerpiece alongside cooler strains like Blue Diamond or Blue Turquoise in a mixed display.

Breeding Notes

Standard discus pairing, spawning, and parental slime-feeding — see the breeding guide for the full cycle. Golden genetics can carry the recessive albino trait without expressing it, so pairing two normally-pigmented Golden fish can still occasionally produce albino offspring if both parents carry the gene — see the Albino guide for more on that trait.

Common Issues

As a solid-color strain, Golden dulls or patches visibly under poor water, thin diet, or chronic stress — a useful early signal, since fading shows up well before a patterned strain would reveal the same problem. Beyond that, it's subject to the same general discus health issues covered in common diseases.

Where to Get Quality Fish

Look for even, vivid gold-orange color with no patchy or dull sections, and confirm eye color if you specifically want the standard (dark-eyed) form rather than the albino version. Pet Zone SD stocks Golden and Sunshine forms when available. See our sourcing guide for general buying advice.

Cost: Typically $20–40 for quality juveniles, in line with other common solid-color farm strains.

Final Thoughts

Golden and Sunshine discus bring genuine warmth and vibrancy to a display tank, rewarding the same water-quality discipline every solid-color strain demands — a strong choice for a keeper who already has discus fundamentals down and wants a bright, high-impact centerpiece.

Golden & Sunshine FAQ

What's the difference between Golden and Sunshine discus?

They're the same strain marketed under two names — a vivid, saturated solid orange-gold farm strain, sometimes called Sunshine or Sunshine Golden at retail.

Can two Golden discus produce albino fry?

Yes, occasionally. Golden genetics can carry the recessive albino trait without expressing it, so pairing two normally-pigmented Golden fish can still produce albino offspring if both parents happen to carry the gene.

How is Golden discus different from Albino Sunshine Golden?

Eye color and saturation. Standard Golden has normal dark eyes and vivid, saturated color; Albino Sunshine Golden is a separate, pastel expression of the same base color with red or pink eyes from a lack of pigment.
