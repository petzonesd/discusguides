Symphysodon spp. — farm strain

Marlboro Red Discus

By Pet Zone SD Team • September 2026

The Essentials
  • Size: 6–8 inches (15–20 cm) as adults
  • Tank: 55 gallons minimum for a group, tall (20"+ height)
  • pH: 6.0–7.2
  • Temperature: 82–86°F (28–30°C)
  • Hardness: Soft to moderate (2–8 dGH)
  • Temperament: Peaceful, shoaling
  • Difficulty: Intermediate to advanced

Overview

Marlboro Red is the other benchmark solid-color strain alongside Blue Diamond — bred for deep, even red saturation across the entire body with essentially no secondary pattern. The name is hobbyist shorthand referencing the intensity of the red rather than any connection to the tobacco brand, and it's stuck for decades because it's simply the most recognizable name for this look.

Like Blue Diamond, this is a strain judged on evenness and saturation rather than pattern complexity, which means it's genuinely more sensitive to visible fading under poor water quality, weak diet, or chronic stress than a patterned strain would show. A dull, blotchy Marlboro Red is one of the clearer "something's wrong" signals in the discus hobby.

Tank Setup & Water

  • Tank: Standard discus baseline — tall, 55 gallons minimum for a growing group.
  • Substrate: Bare-bottom is common among breeders and show keepers chasing peak color and condition.
  • Water chemistry: Standard discus range; keepers targeting show-quality red tend to run especially stable, low-nitrate water.

Feeding

Standard discus diet, with many keepers leaning on beef heart mix and color-enhancing (carotenoid-rich) foods specifically to support red saturation, alongside high-quality discus pellets as the everyday staple.

Temperament & Compatibility

Identical shoaling behavior to other discus strains — peaceful, group-dependent, no special compatibility concerns beyond standard discus tankmate guidance.

Breeding Notes

Standard discus pairing and parental slime-feeding — see the Red Turquoise guide for the full breakdown. Solid-color genetics breed comparatively true, but achieving show-quality even saturation across an entire spawn still takes selective culling over generations.

Where to Get Quality Fish

Look for deep, uniform red with no patchiness or pale patches, and clean, undamaged fins. See our sourcing guide for general buying guidance — this is a strain where source quality shows up fast.

Final Thoughts

Marlboro Red is a striking centerpiece strain best suited to a keeper who already has discus water-quality discipline dialed in — the payoff for consistent care is genuinely one of the most vivid solid-color fish in the freshwater hobby.
